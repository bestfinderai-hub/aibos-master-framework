# DEL 10 — Marketing Intelligence & Content Engine

## 📚 Vision

AIBOS Marketing Intelligence **automatiserar content creation, SEO optimization, och campaign management**:
- **Content Generation** — AI-powered blog posts, email campaigns, landing pages
- **SEO Intelligence** — Keyword research, competitor analysis, ranking tracking
- **Campaign Automation** — Email sequences, A/B testing, performance analytics
- **Social Media** — Multi-channel posting, engagement tracking, influencer matching
- **Lead Nurturing** — Personalized sequences, dynamic content, scoring

## 🎯 Features

### 1. Content Generation
**Blog Posts**:
- Topic suggestions (trending + high-traffic keywords)
- Outline generation
- Full article writing (with citations)
- SEO optimization (headings, meta tags, internal links)
- Publishing workflow (preview, schedule, publish)

**Email Campaigns**:
- Template library (welcome, nurture, re-engagement, etc.)
- Dynamic personalization (name, company, behavior)
- A/B testing (subject line, content, CTA)
- Send time optimization
- Unsubscribe handling

**Landing Pages**:
- Copy generation
- CTA optimization
- Form field recommendations
- Mobile-responsive design
- Conversion tracking

### 2. SEO Intelligence

**Keyword Research**:
- Volume, difficulty, trend data
- Competitor keyword gaps
- Long-tail opportunity identification
- Seasonal trending

**Ranking Tracking**:
- Monitor top 100 keywords daily
- Competitor rankings
- Rank change alerts
- Search intent classification

**Content Optimization**:
- Meta tag suggestions
- Internal linking recommendations
- Readability scoring
- Keyword density analysis
- Latent Semantic Indexing (LSI)

### 3. Campaign Management

**Email Sequences**:
- Trigger-based (signup, purchase, abandon cart)
- Time-based (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Dynamic segmentation
- Conditional branching
- Performance tracking

**Performance Analytics**:
- Open rate, click rate, conversion rate
- Bounce rate, unsubscribe rate
- Revenue per email (RPE)
- A/B test winner selection
- Cohort analysis

### 4. Social Media Automation

**Content Calendar**:
- Auto-posting to multiple platforms (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
- Optimal posting times (per platform, per audience)
- Content variations (video, image, text)
- Hashtag suggestions
- Engagement scheduling

**Monitoring**:
- Mention tracking
- Sentiment analysis
- Influencer identification
- Competitive social tracking

### 5. Lead Scoring & Nurturing

**Scoring Model**:
- Demographic score (company size, industry, location)
- Behavioral score (page views, email opens, clicks)
- Engagement score (time on site, video views)
- Intent score (keyword search, pricing page visits)

**Nurturing Flows**:
- Dynamic content based on score
- Escalation rules (notify sales when ready)
- Reactivation sequences (stale leads)
- Win-back campaigns (churned customers)

## 📊 Metrics & Analytics

### Content Metrics
- Blog traffic (organic, referral, direct)
- Time on page, bounce rate
- Conversion rate (page → email signup)
- Backlinks + domain authority

### Email Metrics
- Deliverability rate
- Open rate (industry benchmark: 20-30%)
- Click rate (industry benchmark: 2-5%)
- Conversion rate
- Unsubscribe rate (target: < 0.5%)

### Campaign Metrics
- CAC per channel (organic, email, paid, social)
- LTV per source
- Attribution (first-touch, last-touch, multi-touch)
- ROI per campaign

### Social Metrics
- Followers, reach, impressions
- Engagement rate
- Viral coefficient
- Share of voice (vs competitors)
