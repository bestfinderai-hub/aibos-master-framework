# DEL 10 — Marketing Intelligence & Content Engine

## 📱 Marketing AI Module

### Purpose
Automated content creation, social media, and growth campaigns.

### Features
- Market research (trends, keywords, audience)
- Content strategy (blog, LinkedIn, video, email)
- Social media automation (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook)
- Campaign analytics (ROI, CTR, conversion)
- Competitor monitoring

### Content Types
- Blog posts (SEO-optimized)
- LinkedIn posts
- Email newsletters
- Case studies
- Whitepapers
- Video ideas

### Tools
- Content Calendar: Plan 12 weeks ahead
- Auto-publish: to LinkedIn, Twitter, blog
- Analytics: Track engagement, clicks, leads
- A/B testing: Test headlines, timing, content
