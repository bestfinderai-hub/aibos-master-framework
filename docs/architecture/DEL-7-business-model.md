# DEL 7 — Business Model & Go-to-Market Strategy

## 📚 Vision

AIBOS är en **platform-as-a-service (PaaS)** för att bygga, driva och skala AI-driven företag.

## 💰 Pricing Strategy

### Tier 1: Starter (Free)
- 1 project, 100 API calls/month
- Community support
- Price: FREE

### Tier 2: Professional (/month)
- 5 projects, 100K API calls/month
- Advanced analytics, Email support
- Price: /month

### Tier 3: Enterprise (Custom)
- Unlimited projects/calls
- White-label, Dedicated support
- SLA: 99.9%
- Pricing: -50K/month

### Tier 4: Reseller Partner
- 50% revenue share

## 🎯 Go-to-Market Phases

### Phase 1: Product-Led (Months 1-3)
Goal: 500+ free users
- Free tier + content marketing
- Community building
- Referral program

### Phase 2: Sales-Led (Months 4-8)
Goal: 100+ Professional subscribers
- Direct outreach + case studies
- Partner integrations + webinars

### Phase 3: Enterprise (Months 9-12)
Goal: 10+ Enterprise, + MRR
- Sales team + white-label partners
- Industry verticals + partnerships
