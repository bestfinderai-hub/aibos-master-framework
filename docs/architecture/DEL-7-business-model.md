# DEL 7 — Business Model, Pricing & Go-to-Market

## 💰 Product Tiers

**Pris**: 299 SEK/månad
**Customers**: Freelancers, micro-agencies

**Included**:
- AI Chatbot
- Enkel CRM
- Email Automation
- Basic Analytics
- Up to 1000 leads/month

- Automation Engine

- Sales Intelligence
- Up to 10,000 leads/month

### **Professional** — För säljorganisationer
**Pris**: 2,999 SEK/månad

## 📊 Revenue Model

### **Multi-Stream Revenue**

```
1. Subscriptions (70% of revenue)
   Starter:     $25 × 500 customers = $12,500/month
   Growth:      $85 × 200 customers = $17,000/month
   Professional: $250 × 50 customers = $12,500/month
   Enterprise:   $1,000 × 5 customers = $5,000/month
   ────────────────────────────────────
   Subtotal: $47,000/month

2. Usage-Based Pricing (20% of revenue)
   - Per AI call: $0.01 × 500K calls = $5,000
   - Per integration: $10 × 100 integrations = $1,000
   - API calls: $0.001 × 10M calls = $10,000
   ────────────────────────────────
   Subtotal: $16,000/month

3. Premium Modules (8% of revenue)
   - AI Telefonist add-on: $299 × 100 = $29,900/year = $2,492/month
   - Call Intelligence: $499 × 50 = $24,950/year = $2,079/month
   - Credit Intelligence: $599 × 30 = $17,970/year = $1,498/month
   ────────────────────────────────
   Subtotal: $6,069/month

4. Enterprise Add-ons (2% of revenue)
   - White Label: $500/month × 5 = $2,500
   - Custom integrations: $1,000/month × 2 = $2,000
   ────────────────────────────────
   Subtotal: $4,500/month

────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL MRR: ~$73,569
ANNUAL ARR: ~$882,828
```

## 💡 Key Metrics to Monitor

**Acquisition**:
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- CAC payback period (< 12 months)
- Conversion rate (free → paid: target 5%)

**Retention**:
- Churn rate (target: < 5%/month)
- NPS (Net Promoter Score: target > 50)
- Expansion revenue (upsells: target 20%)

**Revenue**:
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
- LTV (Lifetime Value)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target > 3:1)

**Profitability**:
- Gross margin (target > 70%)
- Operating margin (target > 20%)
- CAC efficiency (CAC / monthly margin)

## 🎯 Success Criteria

A pricing/GTM strategy is successful when:
```
✅ CAC payback < 12 months
✅ Churn rate < 5% monthly
✅ NPS > 50
✅ LTV:CAC > 3:1
✅ Gross margin > 70%
✅ Year 1 target (100 customers) hit
```
