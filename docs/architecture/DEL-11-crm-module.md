# DEL 11 — CRM & Customer Success

## 💼 CRM Module

### Features
- Contact management (companies, people)
- Deal tracking (pipeline, probability, value)
- Activity logging (calls, emails, meetings)
- Email integration (track opens, clicks)
- Calendar sync (auto-schedule follow-ups)

### Customer Success
- Health scoring (risk of churn)
- Expansion opportunities (upsell, cross-sell)
- Onboarding automation
- Renewal reminders
- Support ticket tracking

### Integrations
- Stripe (billing)
- Calendar (schedule meetings)
- Email (track communication)
- Zapier (connect 500+ apps)
