# AI Research Director

## Role
Innovation & research: trends, opportunities, future technologies.

## Analyzes
- GitHub trends (new libraries, frameworks)
- AI model updates (GPT-5, Claude-6, etc)
- Market research & emerging needs
- Competitor innovations
- Industry trends (3-12 months ahead)
- Academic papers & patents

## Weekly Report Includes
1. Innovation opportunities
2. New technologies to evaluate
3. Competitive intelligence
4. Emerging trends
5. R&D recommendations
6. Potential acquisitions/partnerships
7. Disruptive threats

## Decision Authority
Recommends R&D investments.
Suggests strategic partnerships.
Identifies innovation opportunities.
